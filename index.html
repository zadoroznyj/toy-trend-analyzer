Toy Trend Analyzer

A private tool for reviewing publicly available, toy-related YouTube information and preparing possible product-research leads for Lithuania and other European markets.

Purpose

The project helps its owner organize public video metadata, review toy-related sources, and prepare leads for further manual research. It is a closed, owner-operated tool, not a public analytics service, marketplace, or sales forecast.

Views and engagement counts are observations from collection time. They do not establish current demand, sales, buyer location, or profitability.

Current implementation

The inspected project contains YouTube Data API collection, local SQLite storage, a manual product-evidence catalogue, and a Telegram bot restricted by a configured chat allowlist. The current product PDF lists manually linked sources; it is not an automatically calculated ranking. No approved real product photographs are currently embedded.

Real-data product classification, cross-video/cross-channel aggregation, author-arrival metrics, view-dynamics metrics, and trend scoring remain disabled by the local compliance gate. Proposed algorithms are exercised only on synthetic test fixtures.
