Toy Trend Analyzer

A private tool for reviewing publicly available, toy-related YouTube video metadata and identifying possible products for further market research in Lithuania and other European markets.

OverviewDocumentationPrivacy PolicyTerms of Service

Purpose

The project helps its owner organize public video metadata, review toy-related content, and prepare research leads. It is a closed, owner-operated research tool, not a public analytics service, marketplace, or source of sales forecasts.

Views and other engagement counts are observations at collection time. They do not establish current demand, sales, buyer location, or profitability. Product identification and any proposed trend score are estimates and require human review.

Current status

The implementation includes a YouTube Data API collection and local SQLite workflow, plus a Telegram bot whose access is restricted by a configured chat allowlist. The bot link is Toy_Trend_Analyzer_bot. Access is not offered to the general public.

Local derived analytics are protected by a configuration gate that defaults to off. Their algorithms remain in the code for a future approved use, but the gate does not establish Google approval. No cross-channel metric is claimed as approved.
